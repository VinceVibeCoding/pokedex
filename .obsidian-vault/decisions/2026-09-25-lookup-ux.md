# 2026-09-25 — Lookup UX redesign

Reference: mycollectrics.com is a market-level dashboard (set trends, rarity trends, PSA 10 premium leaderboards). Our niche is the single-card answer: "what's this card worth and what should I pay".

- Search by **name** (people don't know card IDs); card ID stays the API key.
- Answer-first layout: fair price + "to flip, pay up to" (target ROI after fees) + verdict at the top; evidence below.
- Markups above market were the wrong frame for a buyer; replaced by max buy price at 0/10/20/30% ROI.
- Grade buttons show each grade's price and × raw — the grade comparison needs no separate table.
- All grades in one response → grade switching is instant, no request.
